# Files

**Files** keeps your bills, warranties, insurance policies, receipts and photos or videos of what
you own with your budget, next to what they belong to. One file can be linked to as many
transactions, categories, goals and accounts as you like: the fridge's warranty to the fridge
purchase and the extended-warranty payment, say, or the home insurance policy to its Insurance
category and each premium.

Files are kept on your own server with the rest of your budget, and never sent anywhere.

## Adding files

Open **Files** in the sidebar and drop, paste or choose files. Allotment keeps:

- photos (JPEG, PNG, WebP, GIF, HEIC) and PDFs;
- Word, Excel and PowerPoint files, and their OpenDocument equivalents;
- CSV, Markdown and plain text;
- videos (MP4, MOV), for example of your home's contents for an insurance claim.

Each file can be up to 25 MB, and a video up to 250 MB. Allotment checks what each file really is,
whatever its name says, and refuses anything else (programs, web pages, macro-enabled Office files).

You can also add files from where they belong: a transaction's details (**Other files**), a
category's page, a goal's menu (**Files**) and an account's page (**Files**). Files added there
are linked to it straight away.

**From your phone:** install Allotment on your home screen, then share a photo or PDF to it from
another app (Chrome on Android; Safari on iOS doesn't offer this, so use **Add files**, which can
take a photo).

## Finding files

- **Search** finds names, descriptions and labels, and the words inside PDFs, Word, Excel,
  PowerPoint and text files. With [reading receipts](tax.mdx) turned on, **Read its text** has
  your own model read a photo's words too.
- Narrow it down by **kind** of file and by **date**.
- **Starred** files are reminders (a refund still to come); unstar them when done.
- **Important** files are the ones to keep (insurance, mortgage, legal).
- **Labels** sort files into groups you choose. They are the same tags as your transactions.
- **Not linked** shows files you haven't linked to anything yet.

In **Transactions**, a paperclip marks rows with receipts or files, and the **Receipts and files**
filter shows only those, or only those without.

## A file's details

Open a file to see it (photos, videos, PDFs and text show in the page; Office files download), and
to change its name, date, description and labels, star it or mark it important, and see and change
everything it is linked to.

- **Link** links it to a transaction (search by payee or notes), a category, a goal or an account.
- Removing a link keeps the file. **Delete** removes the file and its links, after asking; what it
  was linked to stays as it is.
- **Use as a receipt** turns a photo or PDF into a receipt, so it can be matched and split like
  any other. Receipts appear in Files too, linked to the transaction they're matched to.

At tax time, files linked to a claimed transaction count as its evidence, and go in the year's
download with the receipts.

## Storage and backups

**Storage** (at the bottom of the Files list) shows how much your files take. You can set a limit:
new files are refused once they would take more than that.

Backups of your budget's data carry what each file is and what it's linked to, but not the files
themselves. Your server's own backup (Home Assistant's backup, or the desktop app's data folder)
includes the files. To keep a copy of the files on their own, or move them to another installation,
use **Download all** on the Files page: a ZIP of every file with a list of what each one is.
