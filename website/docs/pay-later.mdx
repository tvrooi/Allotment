# Pay later: Afterpay and other payment plans

When you buy a drill with Afterpay, your bank only sees four "AFTERPAY" payments. **Pay later**
records the drill once, as Home improvement, and links each fortnightly payment to it, so your
spending shows what you bought instead of who you paid.

## Setting up

Open **Pay later** and choose **Set up Afterpay**. Allotment adds an **Afterpay** account (a
small credit account) and an **Afterpay payment** envelope on the Budget page.

## Adding a purchase

Choose **Add a purchase** and fill in where you bought it, what it was for, the price and the
day. Afterpay's usual plan is four payments a fortnight apart, the first taken when you buy, and
that's the default. Pick the account the payments come from, usually your Up account.

The purchase goes on the Afterpay account in its real category. Your Afterpay balance is what you
still owe.

## Confirming payments

When a payment to Afterpay arrives from your bank close to its due date, **Pay later** and
**Review** list it under **Payments to confirm**: "Afterpay payment → Bunnings, 2 of 4". Choose
**Confirm** and the bank payment becomes a transfer to your Afterpay account, so it isn't counted
as spending a second time. **Not this** stops suggesting it. Nothing is linked without you.

Paid ahead? A payment the size of the next two or more instalments is offered for all of them.

If Afterpay's amounts or dates differ from the plan, choose **Change payments** on the plan and
match them, so each payment links.

## Earlier purchases

**Earlier payments** looks back over your bank's Afterpay payments (6, 12 or 24 months) and
groups them into the purchases they probably paid for. Choose **Record purchase** on a group, say
what you bought, and those payments are linked to it. Reconciled payments are left as they are.

## Your budget

- **Bought with money already in the envelope:** that money moves to **Afterpay payment**, and
  each payment uses it up.
- **Bought without it:** the category shows the purchase as card overspending, and the Afterpay
  payment envelope shows what you need to put aside. The Budget page lists the payments due this
  month under it.

## Refunds, late fees and mistakes

- **Refund** records the money back in the purchase's category and takes the last payments down
  first, as Afterpay does.
- **Late fee** records the fee (in Bank Fees, or a category you choose) and adds it to the next
  payment.
- The undo button next to a paid instalment unlinks that payment and puts the bank transaction
  back as it was.
- **Remove plan** deletes the purchase and anything the plan added, and puts the linked payments
  back. Bank transactions are never deleted.
