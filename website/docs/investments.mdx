# Investments

**Investments** lists what your shares & funds, superannuation and crypto accounts hold: shares, ETFs,
managed funds, bonds or coins, with prices you enter yourself. It shows each holding's value and,
when you've recorded what you paid, its unrealized gain.

Holdings are kept apart from your transactions. Adding, pricing or removing one never changes an
account's balance, your budget, net worth or reports.

## Adding a holding

1. Create a shares & funds, superannuation or crypto account on [Accounts](./accounts) if you don't have one.
2. On **Investments**, choose **Add holding**, pick the account and the security, and enter the
   units held. Fractions up to six decimals are fine. If the security isn't listed, choose **Add a
   security that isn't listed** and enter its code, name, type and the currency it's priced in.
3. Choose **Add a price** and enter the price per unit for a day. FlyBudget doesn't fetch quotes:
   update the price whenever you like. Each holding uses the latest price on or before today and
   shows its date; a price more than a week old is marked **Stale**.

A holding without a price shows **No price** and is left out of the totals. It is never counted
as zero.

## Cost and unrealized gain

Open **Purchases and cost** from a holding's ⋮ menu (or select **Unknown** in the Cost basis
column) and add each purchase: the date, the units and the total cost including fees. If you
don't know what some units cost, tick **I don't know what these units cost**; they stay unknown
rather than counting as free.

The gain is shown only when every unit has a known cost. Otherwise the holding says whether costs
are partly known or the purchases add up to a different number of units, and the page totals
leave it out.

The gain is **unrealized and before tax**. Sales, dividends and time-weighted returns aren't
calculated.

## Holdings and the account balance

Each account card compares the holdings' value with the account's recorded balance. When they
differ, **Record as value** opens the account's usual **Update Value** with the holdings' value
filled in. Saving it adds one dated value adjustment, as updating any account's value does.
Nothing changes until you save.

## Other currencies

A security can be priced in another currency than its account or your budget. Values are
converted with the latest [exchange rate](./currencies); without one, the value is left out and
the page says so.
