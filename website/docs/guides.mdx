# Guides and tips

Allotment can show you around itself. Guides highlight one part of a page at a time and explain
it; tips are one-line hints that appear once, where they're useful.

## The welcome tour

A new budget shows **New to Allotment?** at the top of the dashboard. **Take the tour** walks
through the basics in about two minutes: the sidebar, accounts, transactions, Ready to assign,
categories, moving money, Review, recurring bills, the dashboard, hiding amounts, and where to
find help. **Not now** hides the offer; you can take the tour later.

While a guide is open the page behind it is paused, so nothing changes by accident. Use **Next**
and **Back** (or the arrow keys), and **Escape** or **×** to leave at any step.

## Guides for a page

Some pages have their own short guide, starting with **Budget**. The first time you open one, a
line at the top offers it: **Show me** starts it and **No thanks** hides the offer. When a guide
changes enough to be worth another look, it's offered again as an updated guide.

## Finding guides again

- The **?** button at the top of the sidebar (in the menu on a phone) has **Guide for this
  page**, **Take the welcome tour**, **All guides and tips**, and **Read the online guide**.
- On a computer, press **?** at any time to see every guide. Guides you've seen are ticked and
  can be taken again.

## Tips

Tips appear once each, one at a time, in the flow of the page, never over it. **Got it** hides
that tip; **Turn off tips** hides them all. Tips wait until you've taken or turned down the
welcome tour.

## Settings

**Settings → Preferences → Guides and tips** (also at the bottom of the guides list):

- **Show tips:** on or off.
- **Offer a page's guide:** whether a page's guide is offered the first time you open it.
- **Take the welcome tour** and **See all guides**.
- **Reset tips and guides:** every tip and guide shows again, including the welcome tour.

These are saved with your budget, so they follow you to every device you sign in on. In a
shared server with several people, each person has their own.

If you used Allotment before guides existed, the tour isn't offered and tips are off. A note at
the top of the page says where guides are; turn tips on in Preferences if you'd like them.
