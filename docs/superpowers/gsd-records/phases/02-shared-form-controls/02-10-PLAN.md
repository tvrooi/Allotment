<interfaces>
`client/src/components/transactions/CategoryPicker.tsx` today (358 lines, hand-rolled). Read it in
full; these are the rules it enforces:
- Props: `value`, `onChange(id | null)`, `groups`, `onClose`, `position` (`'below'` or `'above'`),
  `transferAccounts`, `fullWidth`, `anchor`, `allowNone` (default true), `noneLabel` (default
  "Uncategorised"). Rendering it means it is open; the caller unmounts it on `onClose`.
- With `anchor` it floats beside that element through a portal, flips above when there is little
  room below, and is at least 16rem wide and at most the screen less 16 px. Without `anchor` it is
  positioned inside its caller's relative wrapper, below or above, 16rem wide or full width on
  phones with `fullWidth`.
- The search field takes focus on open; it is a text input named "Search categories" with the
  placeholder "Search categories...".
- Keyboard order (lines 118 to 128): the no-category row is in the order only when `allowNone` and
  the search is empty; then categories; then transfers. With a search typed the first entry is
  active without an arrow key; with none typed nothing is active. Enter picks the active entry
  only. ArrowDown and ArrowUp stop at the ends.
- The no-category row is rendered whenever `allowNone`, even while searching.
- Matching is lower-cased substring on the category name and on the text "Transfer: name". Hidden
  categories are left out unless chosen.
- "No categories found" shows when no category and no transfer matches.
- Escape on the search field stops there and calls `onClose`; a click outside calls `onClose`;
  clicks inside do not reach the row behind.
- The footer is the "Create new category" button, or the inline form (plan 02-18 rebuilds it).

Its seven users, which are not edited in plans 02-10, 02-17, 02-20 or 02-18:
`payees/DefaultCategoryButton.tsx:43`, `transactions/CategorySelectButton.tsx:70`,

`review/SortUncategorised.tsx:142`, `budget/BudgetSummaryWidget.tsx:362`, `pages/Review.tsx:381`.
