# Budget page critique (9 November 2026)

An impeccable design critique of `/budget` (`client/src/pages/Budget.tsx` and
`client/src/components/budget/`), run on the seeded sample budget at 1512 px and 430 px. Two
independent assessments: a design review, and the impeccable detector (source scan and an
in-browser overlay). Score: **27/40** (Acceptable, top of the band).

The follow-up pass that acted on it is 0.2.0-up.154; see "What was done" at the end.

## Design specificity

The content is specific (recurring debits marked "expected", Rego & CTP, "Until payday", Up
Savers); the form is the standard envelope-app layout (Assigned / Activity / Available columns, a
right rail, ~30 identical green pills). Nothing on the page says "every dollar gets its plot";
the brand colour appears only on Assign and the guide banner.

Detector: source scan clean. In the browser: the "Find a category" placeholder is 3.1:1 (below
4.5:1, at both sizes); headings are close in size (18 px h1 over 16 px body; weight separates
them); the warm page colour and Inter-only type were flagged but are deliberate design-system
choices (false positives).

## What works

- **Expected debits:** "−$1,150 expected" under Activity, a grey bar segment and an
  "Available after these" tooltip. Honest and calm.
- **Keyboard assignment:** Enter, Escape, Arrow and Tab; sums like 100+50; unreadable input is
  refused, never saved as 0; "Fund target" and "Cover overspent" quick amounts.
- **Mobile at 430 px:** no sideways scroll, compact Ready to assign row, 44 px money buttons.

## Priority issues

1. **[P1] `$` and `A$` on one screen.** Rows used the one-argument formatter, which reads the
   budget currency when called; rows drawn before it loaded kept the placeholder `$`.
2. **[P1] Ready to assign buried and coloured as a warning.** Third in the rail under
   "Available", which looks like the main figure; yellow over $23.49 of uncategorised spending.
3. **[P2] The rail is too dense.** Breakdown, cash summary and six $0 rows always visible.
4. **[P2] Every funded envelope is a green pill,** so nothing stands out; states differ only by a
   faint tint; the spent/expected bars have no legend.
5. **[P2] The search placeholder fails contrast** (3.1:1).

## Persona red flags

- **Power user:** no hint that Arrow/Tab move between envelopes; the Assign menu has two items.
- **Keyboard / screen reader:** status by tint alone; "Assigned" column vs "Planned for" name;
  Tab in the editor saves and moves on silently.
- **Phone, one hand:** Auto-assign and "Until next payday" under the top-right ⋯; small pencil;
  income and the month note at the bottom of a long scroll.
- **Up user on payday:** nothing links the deposit to Ready to assign; a yellow "Resolve
  uncategorised spending" greets them.

## Minor

Uppercase section headers; the amount editor's History chart is all red; "Hold money…" and "Put
the rest in…" have equal weight; the first-run banner stays for returning users; reaching $0 to
assign has no moment.

## Questions

- Could funded envelopes look planted and empty ones bare, without decoration?
- On payday, should "$3,476.51 to give a job" be the page title?
- Is a $50k Emergency Fund the same kind of plot as Coffee?

## What was done (0.2.0-up.154)

1. Ready to assign leads the rail, green while its figure holds; other causes are a notice with
   a Review link.
2. Copy: sentence-case headers, "Assigned to", device-aware month card, state-aware Move money
   button.
3. Currency: pages wait for the budget currency on first load; the budget's own currency is
   written plainly (`$`, not `A$`) and a look-alike currency by its code. The owner chose plain
   `$`: the audience is Australian, so it is implied.
4. The rail's working folds under "How this is worked out"; $0 rows are hidden.
5. Pills only for overspent (red) and short/carried (amber); plain figures otherwise; bars have
   a hover label.

Open: a bolder "plots" variant as a separate route, the placeholder contrast, and one
recommended action between Hold money and Put the rest in.
