Connecting Up

Bring in your Spending account, Savers and 2Up with a read-only token.

What you need

An Up account and a personal access token: a long password Up gives you for apps like Allotment. It can only read your accounts; it can’t move money. You can revoke it in Up at any time.

Connect in three steps
Open Up’s developer portal at api.up.com.au and sign in with the Up app on your phone.
Copy the token it shows. It starts with up:yeah:.
In Allotment, choose Connect Up, paste the token, and pick the accounts to bring in.
What comes in
Settled transactions in Australian dollars. Pending ones wait until they settle.
Your Savers and 2Up accounts, if you choose them.
Up’s categories where they match yours; the rest you sort once.
Nothing twice: each Up transaction has a stable ID.
Keeping it up to date

Allotment syncs when it starts and when you choose Sync Now in Settings → Connected banks. You can also turn on background sync there. Each sync fetches recent transactions; all of Up's history is checked once a week, or straight away with Check all history. Your token is stored encrypted and is never included in backups or exports.

Other banks

Any bank: add the account and import the CSV files your bank exports (see Transactions). Allotment can also connect through Plaid, SimpleFIN and Enable Banking (Europe). Those are community supported: they work, but only Up is checked with every release, so if one stops working for your bank, CSV files still do.
