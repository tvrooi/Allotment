Accounts

Everything that holds money, or that you owe on.

On budget and off budget

On-budget accounts (Spending, Savers, 2Up, cash) hold the money you assign to envelopes. Off-budget accounts (super, shares, a car loan) count towards your net worth but not your budget.

Reconciling

Every so often, compare an account’s balance with your bank and choose Reconcile. Reconciled transactions are locked in, so a later import never changes them.
