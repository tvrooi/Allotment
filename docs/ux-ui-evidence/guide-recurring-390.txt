The guide
Bills and pay

Recurring items show what’s due, and what’s coming in.

Add what repeats

Rent, pay, subscriptions, rego: add them under Recurring with how often they happen. The dashboard then shows your next bills and your next pay.

Matching

When a payment arrives from Up, Allotment matches it to its recurring item. If it isn’t sure, it asks you to confirm the match.

Find recurring

Allotment can look through your history for things that repeat and suggest them.

Shared houses

Money your housemates put into a household account is never your income: Monthly keeps it under Household account, out of your totals, and the dashboard’s next pay is only your own. A bill the household shares counts at your share, such as “Your share of Rent”, with the full amount beside it.
