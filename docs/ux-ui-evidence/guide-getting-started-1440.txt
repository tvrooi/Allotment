The guide
Getting started

From an empty budget to every dollar with a job, in about ten minutes.

The idea

Allotment is an envelope budget. Money you already have goes into envelopes (categories) for what it’s for: rent, groceries, the next holiday. When you spend, it comes out of that envelope. If every dollar has a job, you always know what you can spend.

Five steps
Connect Up (or add accounts by hand). See Connecting Up.
Check your transactions. Give anything without a category a home; rules can do it for you next time.
Add your pay and bills as recurring items, so you can see what’s coming.
Assign your money. On Budget, give each envelope an amount until Ready to assign reaches $0. See Budgeting.
Come back every payday. Assign the new pay and move money if plans changed.
Where your data is

Your budget lives on your own computer or your own Home Assistant. There’s no Allotment account and no tracking. Keep a backup: see Backups.
