Currencies

Your budget has one currency. Accounts can each have their own: their amounts stay in it, and the budget and reports count them in yours at each day's exchange rate. How currencies work

Budget currency

What your budget, reports and net worth are counted in. It can be corrected while no account in another currency has transactions.

How amounts are written

On this device. Automatic writes each currency as at home: 1 234,56 zł, $1,234.56, CHF 1'234.56 (the number format is in Preferences). AUD's position is the Preferences one.
