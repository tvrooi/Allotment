Backups

Keep a copy of your budget somewhere safe.

Download a backup

Settings → Data → Download backup saves your whole budget in one file. Keep it somewhere other than the computer or Home Assistant it runs on. Your Up token is never included.

Restore

Settings → Data → Restore Backup replaces the budget with a backup. You’re asked to confirm first.
